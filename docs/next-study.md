directional cues from the p5.js composition. Use its uneven cluster, loose buds,
and curved traces. Do not change the source photograph into a p5.js rendering.

feeling. The slow ambient light continues after the eighteen-second reveal.

The earlier choices below describe the previous revision. The current request

## Decisions

- Use a dense coral centre, overlapping companion clusters, and peripheral buds on ivory.
- Use a generated photographic material with folds, exposed veins, and loose fibres.
- Let light follow specific stems and folds in an authored sequence.
- Let companion groups and buds arrive later. Leave fine hanging fibres until the end.
- Use a precomputed arrival map and a small WebGL shader. Keep the final image still.
- Keep the presentation wordless. No decorative interface or required gesture.

The [clip analysis](motion-reference-analysis.md) remains useful for timing.
The earlier [visual research](visual-direction-research.md) is not a recipe for
the new image. Its literal application produced the rejected copy.

The compared studies are `studies/thread-letter/`, `studies/afterimage/`, and
`studies/exposure/`. The thread study remained too geometric. The procedural
membrane study had too uniform a surface. The exposure study supplies more
irregular material detail and a distinct composition.
