# Sol Herbarium

An intimate bouquet study in translucent woven pigment and metallic drypoint. Curled filament surfaces replace the drawn botanical contours as the flowers form. The dense composition keeps one warm coral lily nested among quieter moonstone, ochre, violet, blush, and blue blooms.

Each petal surface is rendered once into tightly cropped material and glimmer textures. The cache builds incrementally, with the hero flower first, so the paper and drawing appear immediately. Pointer movement gives connected stems a small local lean. A press, touch, or focused `Space` or `Enter` sends one fold-aligned glimmer from the binding through the flowers.

Reduced motion shows the settled artwork and stops the animation loop after the material cache completes. Hidden tabs pause the loop until they become visible again.
